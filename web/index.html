About

Spirits of Bitcoin is a BitcoinCash (BCH) transaction visualizer and sonifier.

The visuals

Each transaction is represented by a spirit. They line up in order of their fee rate (in satoshis per byte), which is visually represented by the color of their lantern. Spirits have one piece of luggage for each output after the first. They have a certificate if they contain CashTokens NFTs, and they hold a coin if they contain fungible CashTokens. They have a scroll if there’s an OP_RETURN output. Larger transactions (in bytes, not amount sent) are represented by larger spirits. Donation transactions have a sparkler instead of a lantern.

Mined blocks are represented by the arrival of a boat which takes spirits to the blockchain, where they will have eternal life.

The audio

All pitches are harmonics of a 30Hz fundamental. Each of a transaction’s outputs sounds its own chime. The frequency of the lowest partial of the chime is determined by the output’s size (in BCH moved). In a logarithmic arrangement, larger amounts result in lower harmonics, down from the thirty first harmonic (dust transactions) to the second harmonic (2,100+ BCH). Then, the pitches of the chime’s other partials are chosen as the pitches in the harmonics of 30Hz that most closely approximate the overtone frequency ratios of an ideal metal bar.
