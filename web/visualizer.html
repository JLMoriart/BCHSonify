The Ferry Line

A moving sketch for the BCHSonify visualizer, running on made-up traffic. Every unconfirmed transaction is a soul waiting in line. When a block is mined, the ferry docks beside the pier and carries that block's souls to eternal life on the chain.

Waiting

A soul drops in from the sky straight to its place, then stands still. The line is ordered by fee per byte, highest at the front. Equal fees keep their arrival order, so most newcomers join the back.

Cutting in

A soul that pays more lands ahead of everyone paying less. The souls behind it each step back one place, and that is the only time a settled soul moves before a block.

The ferry

On a block the ferry docks and the souls in that block light up gold wherever they stand, front of the line or not. They fly to the ferry's lantern, front first. The gaps stay open until the last one is aboard, then the line closes up at a run.

What a soul carries

Lantern
its BCH amount, brighter for bigger outputs
Luggage
one bag per extra output
Crown
holds a CashTokens NFT
Coin
holds fungible CashTokens
Scroll
has an OP_RETURN message
Halo
2,100 BCH or more, the outputs that play harmonic 2

Stand-ins in this sketch

The traffic is simulated: 2,500 souls to start, 4 arrivals a second, a block 8 seconds in and every 45 seconds after. Each block takes 30 to 90% of the line: mostly the front, skipping about 4% of it, plus about 3% picked from anywhere behind. Real BCH blocks usually take nearly everyone.

Counting souls… Drawn live in canvas to show layout and motion. Not final art.
